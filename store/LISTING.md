# Google Play listing — Chronicles of Elderon

Everything the Play Console asks for on the store listing and policy pages,
ready to paste. The images in this folder are regenerated by
`node tools/make-store.js` (see the note at the top of that file); the copy
below is maintained by hand. Character limits are Play's.

## Store listing

**App name** (30 max, 21 used)

    Chronicles of Elderon

**Short description** (80 max, 78 used)

    Tactical RPG: charge-time turns, jobs, height and facing. Offline, no ads.

**Full description** (4000 max)

    Two princes. One crown. A squire who knows too much.

    Chronicles of Elderon is a tactical role-playing game in the tradition of
    the isometric classics. Lead a small company across a seven-chapter
    campaign of battles on height-mapped fields, where you stand and
    which way you face matter as much as what you swing.

    CHARGE-TIME TURNS
    Every unit gains charge each tick and acts at a hundred. Fast units act
    often; a slow spell may land after the battlefield has moved on. The turn
    order is always shown, so you can plan around it.

    HEIGHT AND FACING
    Strike from above for more damage, from the side to halve evasion, from
    behind to land every time. Climb, jump, and turn the field to see what a
    wall is hiding.

    JOBS AND ABILITIES
    Squires become knights, archers, monks, thieves, mages, ninja, dragoons.
    Earn job points in battle, learn abilities, equip a second skillset from
    any job you have studied, and pick your reaction, support and movement
    passives.

    ELEMENTS THAT MATTER
    Fire, ice, thunder, earth, holy and dark. A bomb drinks fire and bursts in
    ice; a fell knight feeds on dark and dreads holy. The targeting preview
    tells you the result before you commit.

    EQUIPMENT YOU CAN SEE
    Buy gear between battles and watch it appear on your characters: the
    sword in hand, the shield on the arm, the helm, the plume.

    MADE FOR THE PHONE
    Tap to move and act, drag to pan, pinch to zoom, turn the field with a
    tap. Play in portrait or landscape. Three difficulty settings, changeable
    at any time.

    NO STRINGS
    No ads. No accounts. No purchases. No internet permission at all: the
    whole game is on your device and plays offline.

## App content (policy declarations)

**Privacy policy**: the URL above.

**Ads**: No, this app does not contain ads.

**App access**: All functionality is available without special access
(no login, no restricted features).

**Content rating** (IARC questionnaire) — answers for the game category:
- Violence: cartoon or fantasy violence against fantasy characters; no
  blood, no gore, no realistic depictions. Characters are defeated and
  "fall"; they can be revived.
- Sexuality, nudity, profanity, drugs, alcohol, tobacco, gambling: none.
- User interaction, sharing of location or personal information, digital
  purchases: none.
- Expected outcome: ESRB Everyone 10+ / PEGI 7 / USK 6 or similar.

**Target audience and content**: choose age groups 13 and over only. The
game is not designed for children, and declaring an audience under 13 pulls
the app into the Families policy with its own review. Do not tick
"appeals to children".

**Data safety**: the app collects no data and shares no data.
- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
- Is all user data encrypted in transit? Not applicable (nothing is sent).
- Do you provide a way for users to request deletion? Not applicable.
The manifest requests no permissions, not even INTERNET, which is what the
review will find when it checks.

**Government app**: No. **Financial features**: none. **Health**: no
health features. **News**: not a news app.

**Advertising ID**: the app does not use the advertising ID.
