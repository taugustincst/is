How to play

  • Deployment: before each battle, pick who fights and where they stand. Units left in reserve take no part. The opposition is listed on the left.
  • Charge Time (CT): every unit gains CT equal to its Speed each tick. At 100 CT it takes a turn. The Turn Order panel forecasts who acts next.
  • Turn: Move, then Act (or Act, then Move), then choose a facing. Skipping Move or Act gives +20 CT toward your next turn.
  • Facing: attacks from the side halve the target's evasion; attacks from behind cannot be evaded. Face your enemies when you Wait.
  • Height: attacking from 2+ tiles above deals 10% more damage; from below, 10% less. Jump limits how high you can climb.
  • Charged abilities: spells and Aim shots resolve after their charge fills. Targets that move out of the area are missed. Friendly fire is real.
  • Falling: a downed unit counts down three turns before it is carried from the field. Revive it in time and it stays. Losing your leader loses the battle.
  • Objective: shown in the bar above. Some battles end when the enemy commander falls, or when you have held out long enough.
  • Jobs: earn JP by acting. Spend it in Formation on abilities and passives. Reach job levels to unlock advanced jobs, and equip a second skillset from any job you have studied.
  • Passives: one reaction, one support and one movement ability at a time. Learn them in one job and wear them in any.
  • Equipment: buy gear at the shop and fit it in Formation, or press Optimize. Weapons set your power and reach; armour, shields and accessories do the rest.
  • Controls: click or tap tiles and buttons, keys 1-9 for menu entries. Right-click or Esc cancels. Drag or pinch to pan and zoom, arrows and +/- as well, 0 to reframe.
