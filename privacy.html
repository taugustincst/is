Privacy Policy

Chronicles of Elderon · last updated 14 September 2026

Chronicles of Elderon is a single-player game. This policy exists because Google Play asks every app to have one, and it is short because there is almost nothing to say.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no network access. The Android app declares no permissions at all, including the permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere even if it wanted to.

What the game stores

Your saved game and two audio preferences (sound on or off, music on or off). They are kept in the app's private storage on your own device, and only there. If your device backs up app data to your Google account, that backup is made by Android under your own account's settings, and the game takes no part in it.

Uninstalling the app deletes the saved game. Inside the game, starting a new game overwrites it.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, children included, because it collects none.

Third parties

There are none. No third-party code runs inside the game.

Changes

If any of the above ever changes, this document will change first, and the date at the top with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's issue tracker at github.com/taugustincst/is/issues.
